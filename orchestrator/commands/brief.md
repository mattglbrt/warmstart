Prepare me for a meeting with the group/project named in the argument.

Read (in this order, stopping when you have enough): the group `STATUS.md`, each relevant project `STATUS.md`, the last 2 files in the group's `meetings/`, open deadlines for it in `PROJECTS.md`, and the top 1–2 entries of the relevant `SESSION_LOG.md` (via subagent if long).

Write `<group>/meetings/YYYY-MM-DD-prep-<slug>.md` with: **Where we are** (5 lines, client-facing framing) · **What we shipped since last meeting** · **Decisions to get** (each with a recommendation) · **Questions to ask** · **Asks/blockers on their side** (name the person) · **Promises we can make / must not make** · **Risks to flag** · a 6-line agenda. Plain language, no hedging paragraphs. Print it and note anything you couldn't verify.
